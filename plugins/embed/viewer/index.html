About this map

Albums from my beets library, placed by how they sound. An audio model (Discogs-EffNet) analyzed each album and converted their sound into high level vectors. Similar-sounding albums sit close together, and lines connect the closest matches.

Select an album to see the calculated numbers. Try the natural language tool to describe a sound to highlight matches. It's neat. Hit the gather button :-)

Phrase search runs locally on my desktop, turning your query into the vectors. Matching against the library happens in your browser.

    How these highlights work

    Completed months compared with the previous three months, using all album arrivals in this export. At least 10 albums in the month, 20 in the comparison, 5 in the highlighted group, and a rise of 5 percentage points. Highlights follow the date window and grouping; shares include hidden groups. First appearances mean first recorded here. These describe discoveries, not repeat listening. Sound labels use current metadata.
